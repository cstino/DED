# D&D Campaign Status: Sharn - Profezia dell'Eterna Notte

## Overview for AI Context
This document serves as a high-level summary of the campaign development for any AI assistant continuing the work. 

**Main Plot**: Lady Illmarrow (Vol) is seeking to fulfill the "Profezia dell'Eterna Notte" to plunge Khorvaire into darkness. This involves orchestrating war and assassinating King Boranel.
**Current Hook**: The characters are investigating a new alchemical drug called **Dragon Blood** (Sangue di Drago), specifically a "pomegranate-red" variant.

## Recent Adventure Adaptation: "Il Palio dei Rioni"
The adventure has been restructured to fit the campaign:
- **Location**: Moves from the Cogs (Ingranaggi) to a hidden laboratory in **Khyber**.
- **Antagonist**: **Zorath, il Tessitore di Incubi**. A Mind Flayer (CR 8), elite member of the Daask and servant of the Daughters of Sora Kell (the Hags of Droaam).
- **Crew Updates**: The crew of the *Thavik's Arrow* now consists of 2 humans (**Runa**, **Jassil**) and 2 elves (**Toric**, **Nym**).
