USING THIS BOOK

Tasha's Cauldron of Everything offers a host of new options for DUNGEONS & DRAGONS, and our journey through those options is accompanied by the notes of the wizard Tasha. Creator of the spell Tasha's hideous laughter, Tasha's life is one of the most storied in the D&D multiverse. Raised by Baba Yaga, the Mother of Witches herself, Tasha adventured across the world of Greyhawk and became the friend and sometimes enemy of other famous adventurers, like Mordenkainen. In time, she ruled as the Witch Queen and later changed her name to lggwilv—a figure of legend who is whispered about, feared, and admired.

CHAPTER 1: CHARACTER OPTIONS

CUSTOMIZING YOUR ORIGIN
Ability Score Increases: You can take any ability score increase you gain in your race or subrace and apply it to an ability score of your choice.
Languages: You can replace each language in your Languages trait with another language from standard D&D lists.
Proficiencies: You can replace skill, tool, or weapon proficiencies with others following the Proficiency Swaps table.

ARTIFICER
Masters of invention, using ingenuity and magic to unlock capabilities in objects.
Subclasses: Alchemist, Armorer, Artillerist, Battle Smith.
Key Features: Magical Tinkering, Spellcasting, Infuse Item, The Right Tool for the Job.

BARBARIAN
Subclasses: Path of the Beast (bestial transformation), Path of Wild Magic (magical surges during rage).
Optional Features: Primal Knowledge, Instinctive Pounce.

BARD
Subclasses: College of Creation (Song of Creation), College of Eloquence (logic and wordplay).
Optional Features: Additional Bard Spells, Magical Inspiration.

CLERIC
Subclasses: Order Domain, Peace Domain, Twilight Domain.
Optional Features: Harness Divine Power, Cantrip Versatility.

DRUID
Subclasses: Circle of Spores, Circle of Stars, Circle of Wildfire.
Optional Features: Wild Companion (summon familiar), Cantrip Versatility.

FIGHTER
Subclasses: Psi Warrior (psionic powers), Rune Knight (giants' runes).
Optional Features: Additional Fighting Styles (Blind Fighting, Interception, etc.), Martial Versatility.

MONK
Subclasses: Way of Mercy (healing and harming), Way of the Astral Self (summoning an astral form).
Optional Features: Dedicated Weapon, Ki-Fueled Attack.

PALADIN
Subclasses: Oath of Glory, Oath of the Watchers.
Optional Features: Additional Paladin Spells, Fighting Style Options.

RANGER
Subclasses: Fey Wanderer, Swarmkeeper.
Optional Features: Deft Explorer, Favored Foe (replaces Favored Enemy), Primal Awareness.

ROGUE
Subclasses: Phantom (connection to death), Soulknife (psychic blades).
Optional Feature: Steady Aim.

SORCERER
Subclasses: Aberrant Mind (alien influence), Clockwork Soul (mechanus logic).
Optional Features: Metamagic Options (Seeking Spell, Transmuted Spell), Sorcerous Versatility.

WARLOCK
Subclasses: The Fathomless (pact with the deep), The Genie (noble genie patron).
Optional Features: Pact of the Talisman, Eldritch Versatility.

WIZARD
Subclasses: Bladesinging (elven tradition), Order of Scribes (sentient spellbooks).
Optional Feature: Cantrip Formulas.

CHAPTER 2: GROUP PATRONS
Group patrons provide a common purpose and perks to an adventuring party.
Examples: Academy, Ancient Being, Aristocrat, Criminal Syndicate, Guild, Military Force, Religious Order, Sovereign.

---

CHAPTER 3: MAGICAL MISCELLANY
New spells and magic items, including magical tattoos.
Key Spells: Blade of Disaster, Booming Blade, Green-Flame Blade, Intellect Fortress, summon spells (Aberration, Beast, Celestial, Construct, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, Shadowspawn, Undead).
Magic Items: Tattoos (Absorbing, Barrier, Blood Fury, Eldritch Claw, etc.), Artifacts (Baba Yaga's Mortar and Pestle, Crook of Rao, Demonomicon of Iggwilv, Teeth of Dahlver-Nar).

CHAPTER 4: DUNGEON MASTER'S TOOLS
Session Zero: Setting boundaries and expectations.
Sidekicks: Simple NPCs that level up with the party (Expert, Healer, Warrior).
Supernatural Regions: Blessed Radiance, Far Realm, Haunted, Infested, Mirror Zone, Psychic Resonance, Unraveling Magic.
Magical Phenomena & Natural Hazards: Eldritch Storms, Emotional Echoes, Enchanted Springs, Puzzles.
